## Council debate

**Round 0 — success question.** Can an independently reviewed system prevent one declared operational role from reconstructing the assembled form before authorized release, preserve evidence of exceptions, and avoid claiming fairness when a receiving centre cheats? Can the proposed schedule serve the actual cohort within regional capacity and accessibility constraints? Each claim needs its own disproof test.

**Round 1 — three options.** The [architecture comparison](../SEALED_PAPER_ARCHITECTURE.md) considers a conventional HSM-backed assembly service, an attested isolated worker with governed key release, and distributed confidential computation/MPC. The first is the simplest reference but exposes decrypted memory to a privileged host. The second is a practical candidate for a synthetic administrator-resistance experiment, with explicit hardware/application/policy trust. The third can change the trust model but does not arrive merely by distributing approval signatures, and full multimedia assembly remains unmeasured engineering work. Keep all three subject to the same bank-access and corrupt-recipient counterexamples.

**Round 2 — opposing arguments.**

- **Advocate:** compartmented authoring, encrypted versioned items, measured assembly, restricted outputs and signed release permits can reduce routine pre-release plaintext paths. A complete custody record can make some substitutions and later stories contestable.
- **Skeptic:** a full-bank insider calculates the draw outside the sealed system; a key-policy administrator may admit a malicious worker; an attested vulnerable program can leak through output or logs. After a correct release, a corrupt invigilator can assist a candidate. Every public commitment may still verify.
- **Concession:** the confidentiality claim must name historical access, privileged roles, hardware and override assumptions. Two-of-three approvals are application authorization, not threshold decryption. A whole-form human review is an explicit early exposure. A simulated worker proves workflow only. Separate pre-release secrecy from leakage to candidates who start later.
- **Operations voice:** simultaneous seats, accessible devices, recovery, centre travel, extended time and staff constrain the schedule. More shifts reduce seat demand but increase form/equating and exposure problems. A near-start draw may leave too little validation or delivery time; a one-hour handoff exposes the form earlier.
- **Concession:** retain both predeclared handoff simulations, publish first plaintext times by role, and require capacity and assessment gates before expansion. A centre or interface that must decrypt early belongs inside the stated exposure model.

**Round 3 — decision.** Prototype the full synthetic sealed path with an ordinary-host reference and an attested worker experiment; retain the existing complete witnessed log and independent verifier. Expand through exam profiles and separately validated delivery adapters. Ethereum stays a shadow evidence experiment. No majority vote among model perspectives establishes a security property.

## Chosen solution

The common protocol binds official exam/session identity, approved content and policy, protected preparation, authorized release, private receipts, exceptions and remedies. Each adopter supplies capacity, exam format, language/accommodation, scoring/equating and legal authority. The first supported delivery integration is CBT; OMR/printing, descriptive, practical and adaptive modes require their own evidence and assessment controls. Generality is a tested interface property, not permission to advertise all modes as secure.

The first security boundary is the [sealed pipeline](../SEALED_PAPER_ARCHITECTURE.md); the second is the [centre and response-custody path](CENTRE_COLLUSION_REVIEW.md). Keep independent evidence and a candidate case route across both. The proposal's G0–G7 gates name evidence, owners and failure actions. Pre-start log scans remain provisional; later complete scans and final post-appeal checkpoints close only the declared history prefix.

## Implementation plan

1. Review the access/visibility matrix and [mock charter](PILOT_CHARTER_DRAFT.md); name all plaintext and policy-changing principals and separate witness, release and cancellation quorums.
2. Build a fresh synthetic bank with item-level assignments, encrypted question packages, separate answers, frozen versions, and explicit historical-access fixtures.
3. Implement deterministic selection, exact signed permits and restricted output in the ordinary-host reference; then test the same workload in selected attested hardware. Preserve the reference's known administrator exposure.
4. Connect one CBT adapter and two simulated receiving endpoints; test early release, invalid recipient, stale epoch, missing approval, diagnostics/backup leakage, collusion and response changes. Neither software simulation nor a valid package passes the full set of gates.
5. Exercise candidate disclosure/complaint/appeal, replay a second synthetic exam profile, and measure capacity, latency and failure handling. Any live-secret step requires independent review and an accountable adopter.

On a failed guard, pause and preserve signed incident evidence. Reuse of the same authorized ciphertext for a retry is distinct from a new draw. A cancellation/replacement must remain linked; no silent plaintext override or alternate seed rescues a failed secrecy claim.
